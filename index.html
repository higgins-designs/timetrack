Hours against contract

Hours are cost, not an amount owed. A design fee is fixed at signature, so hours against a design phase measure what the fee cost to earn. Only RFI responses, submittal review and additional services bill hourly; site visits bill per visit at the contracted rate — never hourly, never derived from distance. Nothing here has been invoiced, and nothing here creates an invoice: Xero is the system of record, and line-item wording is agreed before an invoice exists.

People — admin

NameRoleRate classActive Projects

New sign-ups arrive inactive and see nothing until you switch them on. Rate class is separate from role: role is a permission, rate class is which §5.2 rate their hours cost at ($175 engineer, $90 drafter). It starts unset, and until you set it their hours cannot be costed at all. Staff (admin and employee) see every project automatically, including new ones and the overhead buckets — there is nothing to assign. Contractors see only the projects you assign them, and never the overhead buckets, so assignment is the control that matters for outside help. People who have logged time can be deactivated but never deleted, so their history keeps its attribution.
